# Driver conditioning

Training for sustainable amateur motorsport performance alongside a demanding working life. The vocabulary distinguishes planned work, actual performance, recovery, and deliberate adjustments.

## Language

**Programme**:
The recurring arrangement of strength, power, aerobic, neck, and trunk work for the athlete's current training phase.
_Avoid_: Bodybuilding split, weekly worksheet

**Session prescription**:
The intended exercises, work targets, recovery, and transitions for one training session.
_Avoid_: Completed workout, training result

**Session**:
One actual training occasion, including warm-up, work, recovery, transitions, and stretching. Finishing a session does not imply that every originally prescribed exercise was performed.

**Watch recording**:
An activity recorded on the Apple Watch, with its own timestamps, recorded duration, and available sensor measurements. One training session may contain several Watch recordings, and a recording does not establish which strength sets were performed.
_Avoid_: Completed session prescription

**Recorded workout duration**:
The duration reported for a recorded activity, which may exclude pauses or cover only part of the full training session.
_Avoid_: Full session elapsed time

**Working set**:
A prescribed strength or power effort that contributes to the exercise's working performance record, distinct from preparation sets.

**Warm-up set**:
A preparatory set used to approach working load or rehearse movement quality, not a completed working set.

**Alternating pair**:
Two exercises performed in alternating order with explicit rest after each exercise, allowing recovery before the same movement repeats.
_Avoid_: Nonstop circuit, no-rest superset

**Rest interval**:
Prescribed recovery between working sets, alternating exercises, neck directions, or neck rounds, with its scope explicitly identified.

**Transition allowance**:
Time for moving between exercises, setting up equipment, drinking water, and resetting. It is not automatically zero or interchangeable with protected main-lift recovery.

**Session budget**:
The maximum elapsed time available for the whole session, including preparation, recovery, transitions, and stretching.
_Avoid_: Lifting time, moving time

**Accessory omission**:
An intentional decision not to perform lower-priority work in a session, for time, recovery, or another recorded reason. It is not a performed set or a failed repetition attempt.
_Avoid_: Failed workout

**Per-hand load**:
The load of each individual dumbbell, not the combined load of the pair.

**Reps in reserve (RIR)**:
The athlete's estimate of additional technically good repetitions possible at the end of a set. An unreported estimate is unknown.

**Rep-quality report**:
The athlete's observation of grinding, deterioration, or maintained speed and technique, distinct from the repetitions and load recorded.
_Avoid_: Measured bar velocity, inferred technique score

**Neck round**:
One sequence of forward, backward, left, and right controlled holds with prescribed recovery between directions.

**Zone 2 session**:
The dedicated easy aerobic cycling session, guided here by the ability to speak in complete sentences. The programme label alone does not establish a measured physiological zone.

**Interval phase**:
One hard or easy segment within a cycling interval round, distinct from recovery between lifting sets.

**Recovery day**:
A day without a prescribed training session, not an automatic active-recovery assignment.

**Progression proposal**:
A reviewable change to future training based on actual performance, quality, recovery, and constraints. It is not an instruction to increase load every week.
